I think this analysis does a really good job of pulling in the context of the internet boom and current catapulted growth of artificial intelligence. The Department of Data column at the Washington Post always does a decent job using multiple forms of visualizations, but I really appreciate this story’s approach. The first line graph shows the fastest-growing large majors at colleges and universities across the United States. I like that the author decided to use percentage as the scale on the Y-axis. The next graph does a good job of bringing in the concept of time, making it clear how the most popular college majors ebb and flow. Overall I really like this presentation and don't have many critics.
