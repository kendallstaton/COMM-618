This story is a workforce analysis comparing the slowest growing and fastest growing job sectors in any given year from 1969 to 2025. I find the years picked to be interesting, but the entire framing of this article is "job growth in your lifetime." I think it's safe to presume 1969 goes far enough back to encapsulate most readers full life times. I also think the categories of fastest and slowest job growth lended a hand for the author to stick with these dates, since from 1969-2008 both the fastest and slowest growing job categories were the same. 
I really like the visualization in this story, and the deep explanation as to how this question was raised, though that is pretty consistently engageding in department of data columns. I take slight issue with the proportion of this visualization. The time increments on the lefthand side are not uniform. One section of the chart representing 50 years of data is over twice the size of a section representing 40 years of data. Still, I think the visualization presents the information in an engaging and easy to understand way, it's just that on first glance it may not quite be converying the correct weight of these data points.
