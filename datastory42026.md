First I need to argue my case that this is a data story – because there are no visualizations in it! This story breaks down homicides in the District, because there has been an increased rate over the last week. The author (my colleague Marissa Lang) specifically pulls out data points and trends that emerged this year, then compares them to years past. Don’t tell Marissa, but I think this story is really lacking in its presentation. There are some really interesting data points here that illustrate a small but still growing problem in D.C., but it makes readers work to pull those numbers out themselves. While there has been a spike in homicides over the past few days, the overall homicide rate this year is significantly less than last year. But, without a chart to show that context it's lost quickly. This story is a good example of why visualizations matter and how they make stories easier for readers.
